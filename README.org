support this project through [[https://github.com/sponsors/ccleberg][github sponsors]] or [[https://cleberg.net/tips/][cleberg.net/tips]].

* what
night-sky and space app for iphone, ipad, apple watch and apple vision
pro. swiftui app with a cloudflare workers backend.
https://krz.sh/apps/moonrise/

* does
- the sky for your place and time: about 130,000 stars to magnitude 9,
  all 88 constellations, the milky way, deep-sky objects, comets, the
  planets, and the moon at its true phase
- hold the phone up to see the sky over the camera
- the solar system from above or in 3d, 1958 to 2050, with spacecraft
- the sun now: nasa images, solar wind, aurora forecast, solar cycle
- launches, eclipses, satellite passes, and the deep space network
- a library of 370 pages on planets, moons, stars, constellations,
  deep-sky objects, and spacecraft

* requires
ios 26, watchos 26, or visionos 26.

see =App/README.org=. the worker is in =server/=.
