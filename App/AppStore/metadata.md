# App Store metadata

## Subtitle

Night sky, planets, missions

## Description

Moonrise shows what's in the sky tonight where you are, the planets and spacecraft across the solar system, the Sun right now, and the missions launching next. Everything about the sky is worked out on your iPhone; live data such as launches, space weather and the Deep Space Network comes from public sources.

SKY
- The sky for your location or a chosen city: about 130,000 stars down to magnitude 9, the Milky Way, constellations, planets, the Moon and bright comets
- About 900 galaxies, clusters and nebulae from the Messier and NGC/IC catalogues, drawn with atlas symbols
- Hold the phone up and the chart follows where it points, or see the sky drawn over the camera
- Scrub through the night, or pick any year from 3000 BCE to 3000 CE
- Tonight at a glance: the Moon's phase, which planets are up and when, sunrise and sunset, and passes of the ISS, Tiangong, Hubble and Starlink trains
- Draw your own horizon of trees and buildings
- Constellations from other sky cultures as well as the IAU set
- A red night mode that keeps your eyes dark-adapted

SOLAR SYSTEM
- The planets and spacecraft from 1958 to 2050: the five probes leaving the solar system and today's missions, including Juno, Europa Clipper, JUICE, Psyche, Lucy and Parker Solar Probe
- A 3D solar system with moons, dwarf planets and comets, compressed or at true scale
- Mission stories that move the spacecraft along their path, chapter by chapter

SUN AND SPACE WEATHER
- The Sun as NASA's Solar Dynamics Observatory sees it now, in several wavelengths, with active regions labelled
- The solar wind measured a million and a half kilometres sunward, NOAA's solar wind forecast, the coronagraph and the solar cycle
- The aurora forecast, with an alert when it may be visible from your area
- Sunrise, sunset, day length and the golden and blue hours

LAUNCHES AND NEWS
- Upcoming launches with countdowns on the Lock Screen that update when a launch is held, delayed or scrubbed
- Follow a rocket or a launch provider and each of its countdowns starts on its own an hour before launch
- Space news headlines, with an optional morning digest
- Live coverage of crewed missions to the Moon, with a Live Activity
- The Deep Space Network's dishes and the spacecraft they're talking to
- Local time on Mars at each rover, and their latest raw images

EVENTS AND ECLIPSES
- Eclipses, meteor showers, oppositions and moon phases for the year ahead
- Eclipse maps with local contact times, cloud history along the path, the nearest place to see totality, an exposure guide for photographers and taps on the wrist from Apple Watch
- Meteor shower peaks with the radiant's best time and height and the rate to expect
- Lock Screen Live Activities for geomagnetic storms, meteor shower nights, satellite passes and eclipses

LIBRARY
- 370 pages on planets, moons, stars, constellations, deep-sky objects, spacecraft and rockets
- Planets and moons as globes you can turn
- A timeline of exploration, the scale of the universe, and rockets drawn side by side to scale

OBSERVING
- An observing log saved as plain-text files you can read in the Files app
- Observing programs such as the Messier list, with what's still to see tonight and a PDF of your progress
- Exports for the AAVSO (variable stars), COBS (comets) and the IMO (meteor counts)
- Eyepiece field of view for your telescopes and eyepieces

AND MORE
- An optional daily summary notification
- Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for the Moon, the planets, Kp, the next launch, Voyager 1 and the Sun
- Control Center controls for the Sky, Sun and Launches tabs
- An Apple Watch app and complications

## App Privacy

Data collected, all optional and off until turned on, none linked to the user, none used for tracking:

- Location, Coarse Location: App Functionality. Cloud forecasts send a location rounded to about 10 km to Apple's WeatherKit; aurora alerts send a 5° band of geomagnetic latitude.
- Identifiers, Device ID (a judgement call; Apple's definition names advertising identifiers): App Functionality. The push notification token (aurora and news alerts), the Live Activity push-to-start token (launch countdowns) and the App Attest key.
- Other Data: App Functionality. Time zone offset and which alerts are on; the rockets and launch providers followed.

## App Review notes

No sign-in. On first launch, onboarding explains why location is used and offers Continue (system location prompt) or Choose a city instead; with a city everything works. Alerts and countdown pushes need a device and are off by default. Live Activities can be tried from a launch (Follow on Lock Screen), a satellite pass (bell in Tonight) or a meteor shower in Events.
